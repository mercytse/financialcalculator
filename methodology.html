Mathematical Methodology

Detailed quantitative documentation outlining the core financial algorithms utilized within the simulator engine.

1. Compound Interest & Asset Growth Mechanics

To calculate terminal wealth under discrete compounding periods, the simulator uses the compound interest formulation:

A = P (1 + r / n)^{nt}

Where:

  • A = Final accumulated value of the investment/loan
  • P = Initial principal balance
  • r = Annual nominal interest rate (decimal)
  • n = Number of compounding periods per year (e.g., 12 for monthly)
  • t = Time horizon in years

2. Fixed-Rate Loan Amortization

To determine the fixed periodic payment required to amortize a loan over a set term, the simulator applies the standard annuity formula:

PMT = P × [ r(1 + r)^n ] / [ (1 + r)^n - 1 ]

Where:

  • PMT = Fixed periodic payment amount
  • P = Principal loan balance
  • r = Periodic interest rate (Annual rate divided by payment frequency, e.g., 12)
  • n = Total number of scheduled payments over the loan lifecycle
